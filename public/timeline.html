This is reconstruction scaffolding, not a finished biography. It was seeded from public sources in August 2026 so that the archive work has a frame to hang evidence on. Every entry carries its sources and an honest label: documented means well attested, reported means a secondary source says so, circa means the date is an approximation, and disputed means the sources do not agree. Where we could not verify a day, we did not invent one. Corrections, primary documents, and additions are the point.
