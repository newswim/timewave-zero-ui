Timewave Zero, restored

In the 1970s Terence McKenna derived a "map of novelty" from the King Wen sequence of the I Ching: 384 numbers, nested in themselves at ratio 64, descending to zero — infinite novelty, the eschaton — on December 21, 2012. It ran as software from 1974 until his death in 2000. This instrument is an exact port of that software's mathematics, validated against the original C program.

High is habit, low is novelty. The wave is exactly self-similar: press ⇡64 and the curve you land on is the same shape you left, spanning 64× the time — McKenna's "temporal resonance." At fine zoom the final 384-day cycle resolves into the 64 hexagrams, six days per hexagram, ending on Wei Chi, Before Completion.

Honesty note: the mathematics is real and the fractal is genuine, but "novelty" was never given a measurable definition, the zero date was chosen — then moved to match the Maya calendar — and December 21, 2012 passed without incident. This restoration preserves the artifact, not the prophecy. Read the assessment and the research dossier.
